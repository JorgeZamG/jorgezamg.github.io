AI Decision Framework · Applied in real-world teams
Qualifying AI Projects
Before They Get Built
I worked with cross-functional teams across engineering, business, and operations to design a framework for evaluating which AI workflows were worth building — considering technical feasibility, business impact, and team readiness before committing engineering resources. The goal was to move from ideas to clear, actionable decisions on where AI could actually improve how teams operate.

Evaluation Framework
01

Technical Viability Assessment

Worked with engineering to map data availability, integration complexity, and model requirements. Flagged blockers early to prevent downstream waste.

02

Business Value Modeling

Built ROI estimates for each proposed AI workflow — time saved, error reduction, headcount impact. Translated technical specs into financial language for stakeholder sign-off.

03

Operational Readiness Scoring

Assessed whether the receiving team had the process maturity to absorb an AI workflow — data quality, change management readiness, ownership clarity.

04

Deployment Roadmap Output

Delivered Go/No-Go recommendations with prioritized deployment roadmaps, documented in Confluence and presented to client leadership for GTM planning.

Why This Matters

One of the biggest challenges in AI adoption is not building models — it’s deciding what is actually worth building. This framework helped teams focus on practical, high-impact use cases and avoid wasting time on ideas that wouldn’t translate into real operational improvements.
